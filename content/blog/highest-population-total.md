A handful of countries account for well over half of the world's population. Across 217 countries with 2024 data, **India** tops the table for population total at 1,450,935,791, while **Tuvalu** sits at the bottom on 9,646.

## The top 10

1. **India** — 1,450,935,791
2. **China** — 1,408,975,000
3. **United States** — 340,110,988
4. **Indonesia** — 283,487,931
5. **Pakistan** — 251,269,164
6. **Nigeria** — 232,679,478
7. **Brazil** — 211,998,573
8. **Bangladesh** — 173,562,364
9. **Russian Federation** — 143,533,851
10. **Ethiopia** — 132,059,767

## At the other end of the table

- **Tuvalu** — 9,646
- **Nauru** — 11,947
- **Palau** — 17,695

## What the numbers show

The global average sits at about 37,411,963, with 41 of 217 countries above it and a median of 6,586,476. India reports roughly 150,418× the figure of Tuvalu — a reminder of how unevenly population is spread.
