Over half of humanity now lives in cities—urbanization has doubled since 1950. Across 217 countries with 2024 data, **Bahrain** tops the table for urban population (100 % of population), while **Liechtenstein** sits at the bottom (14.7).

## What the numbers show

The global average sits at about 62.9 % of population, with 113 of 217 countries above it. The median country (63.9) trails the leader by a wide margin. The top country reports roughly 6.8× the figure of the lowest — a reminder of how unevenly urban population is distributed.
