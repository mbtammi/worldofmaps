Japan and Monaco regularly post life expectancies above 84 years. Across 217 countries with 2024 data, **Monaco** tops the table for life expectancy (86.5 years), while **Nigeria** sits at the bottom (54.6).

## The top 10

1. **Monaco** — 86.5
2. **San Marino** — 85.8
3. **Hong Kong SAR, China** — 85.4
4. **Kuwait** — 84.6
5. **Switzerland** — 84.4
6. **Liechtenstein** — 84.2
7. **French Polynesia** — 84.2
8. **Andorra** — 84.2
9. **Sweden** — 84.1
10. **Japan** — 84

## At the other end of the table

- **Nigeria** — 54.6
- **Chad** — 55.2
- **Central African Republic** — 57.7

## What the numbers show

The global average sits at about 74 years, with 114 of 217 countries above it. The median country (74.7) trails the leader by a wide margin. The top country reports roughly 1.6× the figure of the lowest — a reminder of how unevenly life expectancy is distributed.
