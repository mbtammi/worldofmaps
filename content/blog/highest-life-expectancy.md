Japan and Monaco regularly post life expectancies above 84 years. Across 217 countries with 2024 data, **Monaco** tops the table for life expectancy at 86.5 years, while **Nigeria** sits at the bottom on 54.6 years.

## The top 10

1. **Monaco** — 86.5 years
2. **San Marino** — 85.8 years
3. **Hong Kong SAR, China** — 85.4 years
4. **Kuwait** — 84.6 years
5. **Switzerland** — 84.4 years
6. **Liechtenstein** — 84.2 years
7. **French Polynesia** — 84.2 years
8. **Andorra** — 84.2 years
9. **Sweden** — 84.1 years
10. **Japan** — 84 years

## At the other end of the table

- **Nigeria** — 54.6 years
- **Chad** — 55.2 years
- **Central African Republic** — 57.7 years

## What the numbers show

The global average sits at about 74 years, with 114 of 217 countries above it and a median of 74.7 years. Monaco reports roughly 1.6× the figure of Nigeria — a reminder of how unevenly life expectancy is spread.
