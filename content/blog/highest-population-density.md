Monaco packs in over 19,000 people per km²—one of the densest places on Earth. Across 216 countries with 2023 data, **Macao SAR, China** tops the table for population density at 20,570/km², while **Greenland** sits at the bottom on 0.14/km².

## The top 10

1. **Macao SAR, China** — 20,570/km²
2. **Monaco** — 18,693/km²
3. **Singapore** — 8,242/km²
4. **Hong Kong SAR, China** — 7,177/km²
5. **Gibraltar** — 3,847/km²
6. **Bahrain** — 1,971/km²
7. **Maldives** — 1,765/km²
8. **Malta** — 1,727/km²
9. **Bangladesh** — 1,319/km²
10. **Sint Maarten (Dutch part)** — 1,257/km²

## At the other end of the table

- **Greenland** — 0.14/km²
- **Mongolia** — 2.2/km²
- **Australia** — 3.5/km²

## What the numbers show

The global average sits at about 455.5/km², with 26 of 216 countries above it and a median of 95.3/km². Macao SAR, China reports roughly 148,472× the figure of Greenland — a reminder of how unevenly population density is spread.
