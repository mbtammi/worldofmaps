Monaco packs in over 19,000 people per km²—one of the densest places on Earth. Across 216 countries with 2023 data, **Macao SAR, China** tops the table for population density (20,570 people per km²), while **Greenland** sits at the bottom (0.14).

## The top 10

1. **Macao SAR, China** — 20,570
2. **Monaco** — 18,693
3. **Singapore** — 8,242
4. **Hong Kong SAR, China** — 7,177
5. **Gibraltar** — 3,847
6. **Bahrain** — 1,971
7. **Maldives** — 1,765
8. **Malta** — 1,727
9. **Bangladesh** — 1,319
10. **Sint Maarten (Dutch part)** — 1,257

## At the other end of the table

- **Greenland** — 0.14
- **Mongolia** — 2.2
- **Australia** — 3.5

## What the numbers show

The global average sits at about 455.5 people per km², with 26 of 216 countries above it. The median country (95.3) trails the leader by a wide margin. The top country reports roughly 148,472× the figure of the lowest — a reminder of how unevenly population density is distributed.
