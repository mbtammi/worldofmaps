Iceland runs largely on geothermal and hydropower renewables. Across 206 countries with 2022 data, **Congo, Dem. Rep.** tops the table for renewable energy use at 96.3%, while **Turkmenistan** sits at the bottom on 0.1%.

## The top 10

1. **Congo, Dem. Rep.** — 96.3%
2. **Somalia, Fed. Rep.** — 95.4%
3. **Liberia** — 92.8%
4. **Gabon** — 91.3%
5. **Central African Republic** — 90.9%
6. **Uganda** — 90.9%
7. **Ethiopia** — 90.6%
8. **Guinea-Bissau** — 87.4%
9. **Madagascar** — 83.1%
10. **Burundi** — 83%

## At the other end of the table

- **Turkmenistan** — 0.1%
- **Saudi Arabia** — 0.1%
- **Oman** — 0.1%

## What the numbers show

The global average sits at about 29.9%, with 80 of 206 countries above it and a median of 20.5%. Congo, Dem. Rep. reports roughly 963× the figure of Turkmenistan — a reminder of how unevenly renewable energy use is spread.
